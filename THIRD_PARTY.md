# Third-party resources

KineSync-GS interoperates with external research and hardware ecosystems but
does not redistribute their source code or datasets in this repository.

- PyTorch, NumPy, OpenCV, h5py, PyYAML, and MuJoCo are runtime dependencies.
- `piper_sdk`, `python-can`, and `pyrealsense2` are optional hardware dependencies.
- RoboSplat, GS-Playground, ABC, and OpenVLA-OFT assets are configured externally.

Users are responsible for obtaining these resources from their official sources
and complying with their respective licenses and data terms.

The project-page media are rendered evaluation outputs rather than redistributed
source datasets. Embedded third-party robot and simulator assets remain subject
to their upstream licenses.
